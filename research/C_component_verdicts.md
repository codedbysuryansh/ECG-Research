# Step C — Component-by-component novelty & validity verdicts on YOUR original architecture

Legend: **[J]** peer-reviewed reputed journal/conference · **[J-low]** peer-reviewed lower-tier · **[P]** preprint/workshop (report only, not proof). "Verified via" = search-engine abstract/summary (full text blocked in this environment) unless noted.

## N1 — "5-way decomposition of the gap has never been done; prior work splits at most two causes"
**Verdict: FALSE as a method claim. Application-level novelty only.**
- Zhang, Singh, Ghassemi, Joshi, *"Why did the Model Fail?": Attributing Model Performance Changes to Distribution Shifts*, **ICML 2023**, PMLR 202:41550–41578 **[J]** — performance change is a cooperative game whose players are **any set of distributions** (causal mechanisms); importance weighting + **Shapley values**; code github.com/MLforHealth/expl_perf_drop. This is exactly your Stage-1 machinery, for any number of mechanisms.
- Cai, Namkoong, Yadlowsky, *Diagnosing Model Performance Under Distribution Shift* (DISDE), **Operations Research**, online 18 Dec 2025, doi 10.1287/opre.2023.0217 **[J]**.
- Hierarchical decomposition (HDPD), **NeurIPS 2024** (arXiv 2402.14254) **[J]**.
- Imaging: Roschewitz et al. 2024 (2411.07940) **[P]**; Gordaliza et al. 2025 (2512.09094, MedEurIPS workshop) **[P]**.
- Your IEEE Table I compares only against Roschewitz and Gordaliza and claims ">2 causes" is new. A reviewer who knows Zhang et al. ICML 2023 will reject that claim immediately.
- **Validity problem (independent of novelty):** your Component 3 ("benign morphology") is estimated as the FPR gap on confirmed-benign ECGs and Component 4 as the TPR gap on confirmed-disease ECGs. These are *metrics on strata*, not counterfactual "switches", so they are not players in a Shapley game whose values sum to the gap. As written, the "five shares sum exactly to the full gap" claim does not hold for components 3–4.

## N2 — "No one has looked inside an ECG model; SAEs never pointed at an ECG foundation model"
**Verdict: FALSE.**
- Peer-reviewed ECG representation interpretability predates this: van de Leur et al., FactorECG (β-VAE, 21 explainable generative ECG factors, 1.1M ECGs), *Eur Heart J – Digital Health* 3(3):390–404 (2022) **[J]**; follow-ups in EHJ-DH 2022 (doi 10.1093/ehjdh/ztac063) **[J]**; unsupervised ECG representation learning for disease profiling, *npj Digit Med* 2024 (doi 10.1038/s41746-024-01418-9) **[J]**.
- SAEs on ECG foundation models: **ECG-InterpBench** (Duan & Qiu, Rice; arXiv 2607.27404, 29 Jul 2026) — BatchTopK SAEs on 6 frozen ECG FMs × 5 depths × 5 widths × 3 seeds; 49 clinical measurements; MIMIC-IV-ECG replication **[P]**. **CADENCE** (arXiv 2607.25244) — 8,192 "cardiac atoms" from >9M ECG tokens; **targeted ablation of atoms causes selective output changes** **[P]**.
- What is still open: nobody (peer-reviewed or preprint) has used SAEs/causal patching on ECG FMs for **race/ancestry/population**. That is a *question*, not a tool novelty.

## N3 — "No inference-time, label-free correction on any medical time-series model; LEACE never applied to a medical time-series encoder; every existing fix needs retraining"
**Verdict: Mostly FALSE / weak.**
- LEACE itself: Belrose et al., **NeurIPS 2023** **[J]**.
- LEACE on physiological FM embeddings: *The Identity Trap in EEG Foundation Models* (arXiv 2606.06647, June 2026) — LEACE erases a subject-identity axis from EEG FM embeddings and improves clinical decoding **[P]**.
- Post-hoc (no retraining) removal of protected attributes from medical embeddings: *Post-hoc Orthogonalization for Mitigation of Protected Feature Bias in CXR Embeddings* (arXiv 2311.01349; Springer LNCS chapter 2025) **[J-low]**; adversarial debiasing of frozen CT FM embeddings (2502.04386) **[P]**.
- "Every fix needs retraining" is false even in ECG: Kaur et al. *Circ Heart Fail* 2024 **[J]** fixed much of the gap with **group-specific thresholds** (no retraining); Lotter *Nat Commun* 2024 **[J]** cut CXR underdiagnosis bias 46–67% with view-specific thresholds; Kina & Petersen 2026 (MLMI@MICCAI) **[P]** post-hoc prevalence recalibration.
- **Validity problems with the LEACE step (these matter more than novelty):**
  1. LEACE is only **linear** guardedness. An RBF-SVM still recovers LEACE-erased concepts at 70–95% accuracy (reported in follow-up work); kernel erasure protecting against one non-linear adversary leaves the concept readable by another (Ravfogel et al., *EMNLP 2022* "Adversarial Concept Erasure in Kernel Space") **[J]**.
  2. **Erasing demographic information can create new bias.** Petersen, Ferrante, Ganz, Feragen (arXiv 2305.01397, 2023) **[P]**; Parikh, Petersen, … Feragen, *Is invariance all you need for algorithmic fairness? Removing demographic information can create new bias* (arXiv 2609.32004, 25 Sep 2026) **[P]** — mathematically and empirically: when demographics correlate with labels, some encoding is *necessary*; enforcing invariance can hamper mitigation and create new bias.
  3. Precedent that erasure does not close gaps: Yang et al., *Nature Medicine* 2024 **[J]** (shortcut removal gives "locally optimal" fairness that does not transfer); Kim et al., *Diagnostics* 2026 **[J-low]** (race encoded at AUROC 0.83 in CXR FMs but linear *use* ≈ 0.0015; residualisation did not narrow gaps).
  4. In ECG the population signal sits in the **same waveform components that diagnose disease** (QRS voltage; Bollepalli 2025 found QRS is the key race feature) **[J]**, so "remove population, keep pathology" may be physically impossible for exactly the tasks where gaps exist (e.g., LVH). Your own White Sheet recognised this risk ("wrecking-ball entanglement"); the literature now makes it the *expected* outcome, not an edge case.

## N4 — "Shortcut-vs-perception has never been tested; nobody knows if ECG failure is like imaging failure"
**Verdict: PARTLY TRUE for race (no causal test of *use* exists), but the question is already half-answered in a way that weakens your pipeline.**
- Peer-reviewed ECG evidence points to a **score-shift/threshold (prior-like) mechanism**, not perception failure, and to *simple* fixes:
  - Kaur et al., *Circ Heart Fail* 2024 **[J]**: race-specific thresholds improved F1; adding race / per-race models / balancing did not.
  - *Why blinding sex is not enough* — *J Electrocardiol* 99 (Nov–Dec 2026) **[J]**: MI-detection ML had **no sex gap in AUC or calibration**, but female recall at threshold 0.5 was lower; **female scores sat lower within both true classes**; the gap **closed when specificity was equalised**; ECG amplitudes predict sex at AUC 0.845. That is exactly your "reads correctly but a group-linked offset biases the decision" case, shown for **sex**, and fixed by thresholds, not erasure.
  - Imaging: Kina & Petersen 2026 **[P]** "shortcut reliance degrades the classification head rather than the representation"; Lotter, *Nat Commun* 15:7465 (2024) **[J]** thresholds/acquisition fixes cut underdiagnosis bias 46–67%.
- Race-debiasing in ECG already exists (training-time): Ji et al. (Zègre-Hemsey group), *IEEE Trans Biomed Eng* 73(3), Mar 2026, doi 10.1109/TBME.2025.3597527 **[J]** — ACS detection: Black vs non-Black sensitivity gap **9.8% → 1.3%** with adversarial debiasing, AUROC 0.810/0.817. (Also useful premise evidence: a real race gap for ACS.)
- **Hidden problem the literature exposes:** the fix that works (group-specific thresholds) **is explicit race correction**, which medicine is abolishing (NEJM 2020 "Hidden in Plain Sight"; race-free eGFR 2021). Whether a race-linked score shift is *bias* or *correct physiology* **cannot be decided without ground truth that is independent of ECG reading rules**. Your 3-case logic has no way to tell these apart (see "Logic gap" below).

## N5 — "No ECG study separates ancestry from acquisition; the within-hospital multi-ancestry design was never used"
**Verdict: FALSE as a first; and the design assumption is shaky.**
- Within-health-system race analyses are the norm: Noseworthy 2020 (Mayo), Kaur 2024 (Stanford), Hsieh 2026 (MGH), Ji 2026 (US EDs), EchoNext 2025 **[J]**.
- "Same hospital ⇒ acquisition constant" is not safe: in CXR, **acquisition parameters differed by race and drove AI race recognition** (Lotter, *Nat Commun* 2024 **[J]**); 2026 preprints report technical parameters dominate demographic factors in CXR disparities **[P]**. In ECG, care setting (ED vs inpatient vs outpatient), cart model/firmware and year can differ by race within MIMIC/EchoNext; EchoNext even ships `location_setting` and `acquisition_year`. These must be *measured*, not assumed constant.

## N6 — "Cross-ethnic ECG fairness has not been tested; the field says so in print (DA-GAT-v2)"
**Verdict: FALSE.** DA-GAT-v2's sentence is about *its own two datasets* lacking race; it is not a statement about the field. Counter-examples **[J]**: Noseworthy 2020; Kaur 2024; Adedinsewo *Nat Med* 2024 (Nigeria); multisite external validation of AI-ECG low-EF, *JACC: Advances* 2025 (no heterogeneity by sex, race or device manufacturer); Hsieh 2026; Ji 2026 (ACS); EchoNext *Nature* 2025; Korean AI-ECG in UK Biobank, *Sci Rep* 2026. Using the DA-GAT-v2 quote as "nobody has done it" would be caught by any reviewer.

## N7 — "South Asian test cohort from UK Biobank (2,782 people)"
**Verdict: NOT FEASIBLE as written.**
- The UK Biobank *imaging* cohort (where the 12-lead ECGs are) is ~96.6% White; "Asian" 1.1%, Black 0.7% (paired ECG–CMR, n=42,483; arXiv 2411.13602 **[P]** summarising UKB); South Asians are **<1% of imaging attendees** (UK Biobank research story; healthy SA vs White CMR study used **121 matched pairs**, *J Cardiovasc Magn Reson* 2024, PMC11211094 **[J]**).
- Genetic-ancestry group sizes for the *whole* biobank (Pan-UKBB): CSA 8,876; AFR 6,636; EAS 2,709 — not ECG counts. The "2,782" figure in your draft matches none of these and is not the number with ECGs. Realistic South Asian ECG n ≈ several hundred to ~1,000 → underpowered for disease-stratified fairness analysis.
- No public South Asian 12-lead dataset was found (also the previous session's finding).

## N8 — "A pre-registered, every-outcome-publishable design for a bias question is new"
**Verdict: Not a scientific novelty (it is good practice), BUT there is a big strategic opening you did not know about:**
- ***Nature* (main journal) now accepts Registered Reports in ALL fields** — editorial "Nature is expanding Registered Reports to all the fields in which we publish", 27 May 2026 (doi 10.1038/d41586-026-01629-y; PubMed 42203936) **[J-editorial]**. Covers clinical sciences and engineering, and explicitly includes studies "that gather large amounts of data or compare scientific methods". (Since Feb 2023 it had been only cognitive neuroscience / behavioural & social sciences: "Nature welcomes Registered Reports", d41586-023-00506-2.)
- **Condition that matters for you:** Nature's RR page: secondary analyses of existing datasets are welcome **"provided that authors have had no prior access to the data in question"** (self-certification or gatekeeper letter); otherwise a level-based bias-control taxonomy applies. ⇒ **If you run the confirmatory analyses on MIMIC/EchoNext before a Stage-1 submission, you weaken or lose the RR route for those data.** Pilot work must use *different* data (e.g., PTB-XL, CODE-15). This is a research-design fact, not a to-do.
- Background: in RRs ~60% of pre-registered hypotheses are not supported (PLOS Biol 2019, as reported) — editors accept on question importance + method rigour. So for an RR, **the question itself must be Nature-important before any data**; that is the real bar.

## Logic gap in the original 3-case framework (the most important validity problem)
Your cases: (1) AI cannot read the ECG; (2) AI reads correctly but a population-linked bias distorts the answer; (3) AI is right and unbiased. Missing cases that the literature shows are real:
- **(4) The answer key is biased.** The model faithfully learns labels that were themselves produced by race-blind criteria or human readers whose accuracy differs by group (label-choice bias: Obermeyer et al., *Science* 2019 **[J]**; Pierson et al., *Nat Med* 2021 **[J]**: radiologist KL grade explained 9% of racial pain disparity vs 43% for an algorithm trained on patient-reported pain). In ECG, machine statements (e.g., GE 12SL) and LVH voltage criteria are race-blind while their accuracy differs by race (to be verified in Step D).
- **(5) Using population information is *correct*.** If disease truly shows differently (or normal ranges truly differ) across groups, a model that conditions on population signal can be *more* accurate for everyone; erasing it can *create* bias (Parikh et al. 2026 **[P]**; Petersen et al. 2023 **[P]**; Yang *Nat Med* 2024 **[J]**).
- **(6) The "population" signal is the machine.** Acquisition differences by group (Lotter 2024 **[J]**).
**Consequence:** without ground truth that does not come from ECG reading (echo, CMR, outcomes), cases 2, 3, 4 and 5 are **observationally indistinguishable**, and "remove the population signal with LEACE and see if the gap closes" cannot tell you whether you fixed a bias or broke correct physiology. This is the core reason the architecture must change. It is not a matter of taste.

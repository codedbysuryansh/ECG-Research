## What the previous ledger got wrong or missed
1. It missed **Nature's 27 May 2026 expansion of Registered Reports to all fields** (biggest strategic fact for a result-independent Nature paper) and the **"no prior access to the data"** rule.
2. It missed peer-reviewed ECG race-debiasing work (Ji, *IEEE TBME* 2026) and the *J Electrocardiol* 2026 sex paper (equal AUC/calibration, unequal operation; fixed by equalising specificity) — both directly relevant to your "reads correctly but biased" case.
3. It presented "the label decides" as new; the clinical half of it (race-blind LVH criteria misfire by race vs echo) is from **1992–2002**. What would be new is showing how this propagates into foundation models and whether models covertly correct it.
4. It did not say clearly *why* your architecture had to change. The reason is the logic gap: without ground truth, "bias", "correct physiology" and "biased labels" look the same (see C_component_verdicts.md).

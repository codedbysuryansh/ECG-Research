## E.1 New scientific tension found in this session (not in previous ledger)
**Is the race signal in the ECG biology, environment, or artifact?** Reputed sources disagree:
- Bollepalli et al., *npj Cardiovasc Health* 2025 [J]: signal emerges after birth (AUC 0.59 → 0.84 by 18 y), varies with income → "non-genetic".
- **Dallas Heart Study**, *JAMA Cardiology* 2019 (PubMed 30427995) [J]: in 2,077 Black/White adults, **genetically inferred African ancestry — not self-reported race — was associated with higher ECG voltage AND CMR-measured concentric LV remodelling**.
- BioVU, *BioData Mining* 2015 [J]: European ancestry proportion ↔ longer QRS in African Americans.
- Clinical: obesity markedly attenuates ECG-LVH criteria validity in Black Africans; BMI-corrected voltage improves detection (*Clin Cardiol* 2016; PubMed 23169235, 27279262) [J]; MESA ECG-LVH index best in African Americans vs CMR [J].
**Implication:** the "benign variant" story in your drafts is **not settled**. Part of the higher voltage may reflect *real* concentric remodelling (not benign), part body habitus, part environment. A model that "corrects away" voltage by race could **hide real disease** — the same trap as race-corrected eGFR. This is exactly why removing the population signal cannot be judged without ground truth.

## E.2 Template from medicine: the pulse-oximeter precedent
Sjoding et al., *NEJM* 2020 (occult hypoxemia: pulse oximetry overestimates SpO₂ in Black patients **versus arterial blood gas truth**) [J] → changed FDA guidance. The decisive design was **device reading vs independent physiological truth, by race**. The ECG-AI analogue (AI reading vs echo/CMR/outcome truth, by population, with a causal test of *why*) has not been done (searched: "AI-ECG LVH race echo", "truth-anchored race audit ECG", "covert race correction ECG"). Closest: race-stratified AUROCs on EchoNext in 2026 preprints (2603.28532, 2603.02616) — behavioural only, AUROC only.

## E.3 More facts that shape the design
- **India / South Asia:** Macfarlane et al., "Normal limits of the electrocardiogram in Indians", *J Electrocardiol* 2015 (PubMed 25990450) [J]: 963 healthy adults in India; findings "paralleled those in other populations"; **"diagnostic criteria currently used for Caucasians need not be altered for use in South Asians."** ⇒ the specific premise "Western ECG rules misread benign Indian ECGs" is *not* supported by the best normal-limits evidence. (Real-world AI-ECG deployment in India exists: Project Heart Beat hub-and-spoke, PMC13039740 [J]; KERALA-AF is stroke prediction, not bias.)
- **Race-aware vs race-blind ECG standards coexist today:** GE 12SL is race-blind (age/sex only) [manufacturer guide]; the **Glasgow (Uni-G) program accepts race and applies race-specific normal limits** (Caucasian, Chinese, African; separate studies in Indians, Nigerians, Angolans) [J: *J Electrocardiol*; CinC]. The **AHA/ACCF/HRS 2009 ECG standardisation recommendations** state criteria *should* be adjusted for gender, race and body habitus when validated [J]. Meanwhile medicine is removing race from eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) and spirometry (race-neutral GLI Global, ATS 2023; PMC10236239) [J]. ⇒ ECG is the one major test where the "race correction" question is still unresolved **and** the AI now reads race directly from the waveform.
- **Label provenance differs by dataset:** HEEDB/ECGFounder labels came from 12SL-assisted physician annotation (race-blind); CODE (Brazil) labels combine Glasgow/Uni-G statements with cardiologist reports; MIMIC-IV-ECG machine reports come from **mixed devices (Burdick/Spacelabs, Philips, GE)** — so "same hospital" ≠ same acquisition or same rule-set.
- **AUROC parity is already common:** EchoNext AUROC 0.84–0.86 across race/ethnicity (dataset: 31% Hispanic, 29.2% White, 16.5% Black, 3.4% Asian); MGH FM trained White-only kept AUROC ≥0.85 in all groups (Hsieh 2026). ⇒ Another subgroup-AUROC audit adds nothing. The open question is *how* parity (or disparity) is produced and whether population use is right vs truth.
- **Location-dependent physiology is real but data-poor:** high-altitude Tibetan vs Han ECG differences (*J Racial Ethn Health Disparities* 2025) [J]; no public datasets.
- **No ECG→socioeconomic-status prediction paper found** (searched); Bollepalli 2025 shows SES modulates the race signal. Ethically fraught; keep as an analysis variable, not a headline.
- **SAE ablation to remove bias while keeping demographic recognition** exists for LLMs (Shan & Mueller, arXiv 2512.20796, Dec 2025 [P]; documents "prior collapse") → tool not novel; ECG/physiology application + truth-anchoring would be.
